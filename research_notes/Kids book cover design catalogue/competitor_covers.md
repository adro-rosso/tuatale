# Competitor cover design: personalised children's-book brands (name + likeness on the cover)

Scope: FRONT COVERS only, current (2022-2026) designs as shown on the brands' own product/catalogue pages on 2026-10-23, plus notable earlier designs. Observations marked "(observed)" are direct visual inspections of the brand's own product-page cover renders made in the browser on the cited URL; everything else is quoted from the cited page. Brands covered: Wonderbly, Hooray Heroes, Librio, I See Me!, My Magic Story, Dinkleboo, In The Book, Put Me In The Story, Identity Direct / "Lovable Me", Hallmark, plus Hippo Blue (AU) and Penwizard (UK) as incidental finds.

## Brand-by-brand cover catalogue (reference material for every key question below)

### Wonderbly (formerly Lost My Name; UK; owned by Penguin Random House since June 2025)

#### Takeaway
Wonderbly runs a "name-IN-the-title" house style: the child's name is set as part of the title lettering on nearly every cover, art is full-bleed hand-drawn illustration, and each title has its own bespoke title lettering (no shared template); the one famous exception is the original Lost My Name cover, which carries NO name at all because the name is the story's end reveal.

#### Cited Findings
- Catalogue scale and format claims: "221 books"; Lost My Name product page says "Each book is A4-sized (30cm x 21cm), making it ideal for bedtime stories and shared reading sessions. Printed on thick, quality paper and available in softcover or hardcover." (Size & quality accordion) — [Wonderbly, Lost My Name UK product page](https://www.wonderbly.com/uk/personalized-products/lost-my-name-v2-book)
- Lost My Name cover (observed): full-bleed sky illustration (girl edition: teal-blue sky; boy edition: purple sky), hand-brushed white lettering "The little girl who lost her Name" / "The little Boy who lost His Name" occupying the upper two-thirds, with "Name" set much larger than the rest; a tiny red biplane and rainbow-striped hills along the bottom; NO child's name and NO character on the front. The product-page carousel is labelled "different cover colours of the book" and "The little girl who lost her name purple cover" — [Wonderbly, Lost My Name UK product page](https://www.wonderbly.com/uk/personalized-products/lost-my-name-v2-book); alt-text via [Wonderbly, lost-my-name-book page](https://wonderbly.com/uk/personalized-products/lost-my-name-book)
- Wonderbly's own copy explains why the name is withheld: "And the big reveal of their name at the end? It's the final touch that every storytime needs." and "Add a child's first name, and see each letter become a character in their book." — [Wonderbly, Lost My Name UK product page](https://www.wonderbly.com/uk/personalized-products/lost-my-name-v2-book)
- Catalogue grid covers (observed on the all-books page, each a square-format thumbnail with the title lettering drawn as part of the art):
  - "When Jack Was Born" — cream/parchment ground, soft serif-script title in three stacked lines ("When / Jack / Was Born") with the name on its own line in the same face but larger, a watercolour lion and sleeping baby below. Product page: "Choose one of four covers, each with a watercolour illustration." — [Wonderbly all books UK](https://www.wonderbly.com/uk/personalized-books); [When You Were Born page](https://www.wonderbly.com/uk/personalized-products/when-you-were-born-book)
  - "Liam and Hazel Together at Christmas" — names set as the first line of the title in a red rounded script, festive two-child illustration (sibling book).
  - "I SPY FREYA!" — bold condensed white caps with heavy outline on a red/orange ground, magnifying-glass motif, name as the loudest word.
  - "Rosie's Treasury of Nursery Rhymes" — possessive-name title ("Rosie's") in a mustard script over a dense cream-ground illustration.
  - "Ava's BIG SISTER Olivia" — near-empty cream cover, small pink-brown serif caps title with both names, two small drawn figures; the most "minimal/premium" cover in the range.
  - "CHARLIE & OLIVIA The Search for Santa" — red/white bold caps, names on the top line, busy search-and-find scene.
  - "I'M AN ISABELLA-O-SAURUS!" — white outlined caps, name fused into the pun title; dinosaur scene.
  - "WHERE ARE YOU ARCHIE, EMILY & FREYA?" — three names inside the title on a green ground, three avatar heads in a magnifying circle.
  — all [Wonderbly all books UK](https://www.wonderbly.com/uk/personalized-books)
- Cover colour choice is a standard lever: "Favourite colour for the cover: choose from Sea Blue, Vanilla, Strawberry or Sunshine" and "The square format (22 x 22cm) and thick, high-quality paper ... Choose from softcover or hardcover" (I Love You This Much) — [Not On The High Street, Wonderbly listing](https://Notonthehighstreet.Com/wonderbly/product/i-love-you-this-much-personalised-picturebook-gift); "Each book gives you cover color options to choose from so you can select your favorite!" and "the children's names and adventurers also appear on the book's cover, making each copy unique." — [Baby Chick](https://www.baby-chick.com/wonderbly-personalized-books-for-dads-on-fathers-day/)
- Format tiers: "choose from three different covers, hardback, softcover or softcover keepsake which includes a gift box" — [Playdays and Runways](https://www.playdaysandrunways.co.uk/2020/06/personalised-books-from-wonderbly.html); US price/size tiers reported as softcover 8 x 10 in, hardcover 8 x 10 in, layflat hardcover 11 x 13 in; "A different cover design adds roughly $5-10 to the base price" — [Greenlit Content review](https://greenlitcontent.com/resources/wonderbly-review) (third-party; conflicts with the 30x21 cm and 22x22 cm figures above — sizes clearly vary by title)
- Design rationale from founders: they wanted a book "genuinely personalized, rather than slapping a child's name on it"; "the unique and remarkable illustration style in our books makes us stand out"; "we are a design company at the end of the day" — [Canva Design School, How Wonderbly sold 3 million books](https://www.canva.com/learn/how-wonderbly-sold-3-million-books/)
- In-house illustrator on cover production: for *Y is For You* the cover needed "thousands of assets," including each letter and special characters, and adding avatars and cover-colour options multiplied the work; style is tuned per title ("softer, textural" vs "bold and vibrant") — [Creative Lives in Progress, Adam Hancher](https://www.creativelivesinprogress.com/article/adam-hancher)
- Name length: "all of Wonderbly's story books are able to be customized for any name, however long or short" — [Sago Mini blog](https://sagomini.com/article/whats-in-a-name-5-reasons-we-love-wonderblys-customizable-storybooks/) (secondary claim)
- Corporate: rebranded from Lost My Name in 2017; owned by Penguin Random House as of June 2025 — [Wikipedia, Wonderbly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wonderbly)

#### Inferences
- Wonderbly's covers vary per title in lettering and layout but share a system: square-ish format, hand-rendered title that incorporates the name, no badges or banners, brand mark not visible on the front. The "premium" feel comes from bespoke lettering + white/cream space (e.g. Big Sister, When You Were Born) rather than from framing devices.
- Multi-name titles are handled by writing the names into the title line (ampersand/comma lists), which gets visibly denser at three names.

#### Gaps
- Wonderbly does not publish per-title cover dimensions in text; the A4 (30x21) claim on Lost My Name vs 22x22 cm on other listings could not be reconciled from official sources.
- No official statement on name-length limits or auto-fit behaviour for cover lettering.

### Hooray Heroes (Slovenia/USA)

#### Takeaway
Hooray Heroes is the clearest LANDSCAPE example (12 x 8.5 in), with a 3D-render cartoon avatar dominating the cover, the child's name set as a separate script/rounded word above or inside giant extruded display type, and a per-title (not shared) layout.

#### Cited Findings
- Format: "No. of pages: 28 pages; Binding: Soft or hard; Format: 12 in. x 8.5 in. landscape" (identical spec on at least two titles); "available in soft or hard cover"; "Your child's character and name appear on every page" — [Hooray Heroes, Lucy Rocks](https://hoorayheroes.com/products/custom-gift-for-kid); [Hooray Heroes, You'll Always Be My Little Girl](https://hoorayheroes.com/products/personalized-book-for-dad)
- "Lucy Rocks" cover (observed, landscape): pale-blue sky ground with comic "splash" bursts top-left/right; "LUCY" small in pink rounded caps centred near the top; "ROCKS" enormous in pink, bevelled/3D-extruded caps with star confetti; the avatar is a close-up face peeking up from the bottom edge (eyes, fringe, hands on the frame), occupying the bottom half; a tiny Hooray Heroes badge top-left. — [Hooray Heroes, Lucy Rocks](https://hoorayheroes.com/products/custom-gift-for-kid)
- "You'll Always Be My Little Girl" cover (observed, landscape): white/pastel ground, dad full-figure carrying child on shoulders (3D-render style), monkey plush; on the right, "Amelia" in a pink brush script above the title stacked in pastel gradient caps "YOU'LL ALWAYS BE MY LITTLE girl"; no banner. — [Hooray Heroes, You'll Always Be My Little Girl](https://hoorayheroes.com/products/personalized-book-for-dad)
- Other covers (observed, catalogue grid): "The Pawsome Adventures of CAMILA & WINSTON" — title in white rounded bubble letters on a ribbon-like band with the two names on the band's lower line; child + dog avatars. "Archie's FIRST CHRISTMAS" — possessive name small, "FIRST CHRISTMAS" in red/green 3D letters with baby avatar. "Henry's FIRST BIRTHDAY" — similar. "Roxy was the GOODEST girl" (pet memorial) — pet name in script, big pet face. "WHEN MIA ..." (growing-up book). — [Hooray Heroes, All Books](https://hoorayheroes.com/collections/all-books)
- Likeness system: "design the characters in mind-blowing detail ... Skin tone, hair style & color, eye shape & color, and you even have the option to add glasses and/or freckles ... Hero Palette™ personalizer"; "No AI - No Shortcuts"; "Over 3 Million Books Sold" — [Hooray Heroes home](https://hoorayheroes.com/)
- Customer themes (Trustpilot 4.3, 7,493 reviews): praise "Quality is amazing - such vivid colours!"; complaints "the skin colors were COMPLETELY INCORRECT. As well as messed up hairstyles." (1★, May 2026) and "The print color of the dedication is white on a light blue background, which hardly shows up at all." (1★, Feb 2026) — [Trustpilot, Hooray Heroes](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/hoorayheroes.com)
- Third-party feedback summary: customers "desire more hairstyle choices" — [Kimola report summary via search](https://kimola.com/reports/unlock-insights-with-our-hooray-heroes-customer-feedback-report-trustpilot-en-us-149358) (page itself now returns 410 Gone)

#### Inferences
- Hooray Heroes treats the avatar as the hero image (face fills 40-60% of the cover) and the name as a secondary lockup to a fixed, pre-drawn title word; this lets the title typography stay heavy and 3D while the variable name is a lighter script that can scale without breaking the composition.

#### Gaps
- No published name-length limit or description of how long names are fitted on the cover.

### Librio (Switzerland/UK)

#### Takeaway
Librio uses hand-lettered or bold sans titles with the name either fused into the title ("WHERE'S DAISY?", "ENZO & DADDY") or on a secondary line ("WELCOME TO THE WORLD / EMMA", "A story for Sebastian", "A TALE OF TWO with POPPY & ALBIE"), always with the avatar visible on the front; a dark circular badge/porthole device recurs on the search-and-find covers.

#### Cited Findings
- Format: "Softback: 25.8 cm x 21.5 cm (10.15 in x 8.46)"; "Hardback: 26.5 cm x 22.2 cm (10.43 in x 8.74)"; "Jumbo" 30.8 x 25.8 cm for the European search-and-find; covers have "a smooth soft-touch coating"; "The child's image and name appear on the cover." — [Librio help, What are Librio's books like?](https://help.librio.com/article/22-books-specifications)
- Name length: "The allocated space for the name is unfortunately limited and cannot be changed." "Occasionally, double-names can be too long to fit in the space provided." Workaround: first name only; "With the dedication there is more room for creativity and here you can put the full double-name." — [Librio help, Why can't I add the full name?](https://help.librio.com/article/240-why-cant-i-add-the-full-name)
- Configurable: "you configure the hero's name and appearance and the language of the book" plus a dedication at the front — [Librio help, What can I configure](https://help.librio.com/article/29-books-configure)
- "My Search-and-Find World Trip" cover (observed, portrait): "WHERE'S DAISY?" / "WHERE'S JACK?" in white rounded caps inside a dark-teal circle (globe) centred on the cover; a small circular portrait of the avatar's head sits above the circle; the full-bleed background is a busy search-and-find landscape; a thin subtitle line "my search-and-find world trip" under the name. Copy: "Choose from over 750 combinations including clothing and hat color as well as glasses." — [Librio, World Trip](https://uk.librio.com/en/personalised-childrens-books/my-search-and-find-world-trip)
- "The Tree, The Key & Me" cover (observed, portrait): sky-blue ground, green hand-lettered title "The TREE the KEY & ME" top-centre, then a smaller dedication-style line "A story for Sebastian", a tree with a face at left, the child's avatar as a small full-figure walking at lower right, author/illustrator credit at the bottom. — [Librio, The Tree, The Key & Me](https://uk.librio.com/en/personalised-childrens-books/the-tree-the-key-and-me)
- "Welcome to the World" cover (observed): teal ground, "WELCOME TO THE WORLD" in white caps, name "EMMA" in pink caps directly beneath at larger size, dragon illustration lower half; small subtitle "(it's a strange place)". — [Librio, Welcome to the World](https://uk.librio.com/en/personalised-childrens-books/welcome-to-the-world)
- "A Tale of Two" cover (observed): teal bookshelf background, "A TALE OF TWO" in white hand caps, then "with POPPY & ALBIE" in orange caps, two avatars peeking from the shelves. — [Librio, A Tale of Two](https://uk.librio.com/en/personalised-childrens-books/a-tale-of-two)
- "Lost for Words" cover (observed): white ground, leaf border, "ENZO & DADDY" in two-colour bold sans caps (green/orange), "LOST FOR WORDS" small beneath, father + child avatars; the two-child edition reads "HENRY, AMELIA & DADDY". — [Librio products](https://uk.librio.com/en/products)
- Cover-design rationale (search-and-find cover blog): brief was "Show the child and their name prominently", "Find a way of combining bold, simple design with the complexity of a search-and-find illustrations", "Resemble the first cover, but not too much"; art director: "the title and table need to be centred and less high"; feedback "the child is looking away, they should be looking more to the front"; muted colours were pushed brighter and the team ended up "offering the cover in multiple colour options"; survey of fans preferred it to a "traditional" search-and-find layout; result described as "modern, fresh and exciting". — [Librio blog, Always judge a book by its cover](https://blog.librio.com/always-judge-a-book-by-its-cover/)
- Review praise/criticism: The Bump named Librio "Welcome to the World" its overall best personalised book — [The Bump](https://thebump.com/a/best-personalized-childrens-books); Book Riot: Librio "offered the most varied customization" and its search-and-find "turns the cover into a 'Where's [your child's name]'" title — [Book Riot](https://bookriot.com/best-personalized-childrens-books/); reviewer noted ethnic range "was limited" — [What's Good To Do review](https://whatsgoodtodo.com/the-tree-the-key-me-personalised-book-review/); customer: "Beautifully made book. Colours are amazing" — [Librio World Trip reviews](https://uk.librio.com/en/personalised-childrens-books/my-search-and-find-world-trip)

#### Inferences
- Librio is the only brand found that (a) publicly documents a fixed name slot and (b) uses a true "for <name>" dedication line on the front cover (Tree/Key/Me). Its circle-badge device and portrait format are the closest analogue to a "badge" template.

#### Gaps
- Librio does not state orientation explicitly; the dimensions (W x H 21.5 x 25.8) and screenshots show portrait.

### I See Me! (USA; AU store prints in Australia)

#### Takeaway
I See Me! is the "classic storybook" template: portrait 22 x 28 cm hardcover, painterly traditional illustration, child's name set in the title as a possessive in gold/yellow serif caps ("TAYLOR'S VERY OWN NAME"), author/illustrator credit on the front, and alternative "cover editions" of the same story.

#### Cited Findings
- Format and cover editions: "Ages 0-6; 22 x 28 cm; 44 pages; Printed in Australia"; "Our bestselling story now comes with another cover option, featuring a giraffe!"; product is titled "My Very Own Name Classic Cover Edition" and a sibling SKU "My Very Own Name Personalised Book" (giraffe). — [I See Me AU, My Very Own Name Classic Cover Edition](https://www.iseeme.com/en-au/my-very-own-name-personalised-book.html)
- Classic cover (observed): deep-blue sky, title "TAYLOR'S VERY OWN NAME" in gold/yellow serif caps across the top two lines, a realistic painted owl in flight centre, green valley below, small white credit line at the bottom "Written by ... Illustrated by ..."; no banner or badge; name is the first word of the title. — same page
- Photo-on-cover option exists on some titles: I'm an All-Star "comes personalized with your child's name on the cover and throughout the book, and the child's photo can optionally go on the cover too" — [Macaroni Kid review via search](https://rockville.macaronikid.com/articles/58264fb924d8f4da3aaf5828/im-an-all-star-personalized-book-from-i-see-me-review)
- Name limits on older "My Very Own" titles: 15 characters first+middle, additional 15 for last name — [The Old Schoolhouse review via search](https://www.theoldschoolhouse.com/product-reviews/?rid=2816); Hello World! instructions "ONLY include the child's first name and not add an apostrophe-s" — [Maisonette listing](https://www.maisonette.com/product/hello-world-personalized-baby-book-blue)
- Range: Storybooks, Board Books, Photo Books; "Add a few personal details / See your personalised book come to life / Watch their face light up!" — [I See Me home](https://www.iseeme.com/)

#### Inferences
- The "Classic Cover Edition" naming suggests I See Me! sells cover variants as a feature, i.e. one story with multiple framed cover artworks (owl vs giraffe), which is the opposite of Wonderbly's one-cover-many-colours model.

#### Gaps
- Could not capture additional I See Me! cover renders (lazy-loaded thumbnails plus cookie modal); AU catalogue names observed include "My Very Own Fairy Tale Classic Cover Edition", "ABC What I Can Be!", "My Royal Birthday Adventure", "Hello World! board book (pink/blue)".

### Dinkleboo (Australia/US)

#### Takeaway
Dinkleboo is the value end: 20x20 or 25x25 cm square, gloss-laminated, 18 pages, bold cartoon avatars, name always in the title ("DAVID the MONSTER TRUCK", "Where's Noah?", "Amelia's 1st Birthday", "First Christmas for Charlotte"), and the only brand with documented customer complaints about long names being "squashed" on the typeset.

#### Cited Findings
- Format: "Standard is 20x20cm (8x8"). Large is 25x25cm (10x10")"; "Standard Soft Cover, Standard Hard Cover, Large Soft Cover, and Large Hard Cover"; "18 pages"; "150gsm for the inside pages and 300gsm for the cover"; "Gloss coating"; the main character's name "will appear on the cover & throughout the story." — [Dinkleboo AU, The Monster Truck](https://www.dinkleboo.com.au/products/the-monster-truck-personalised-story-book)
- Covers (observed): "DAVID the MONSTER TRUCK" — name in giant red extruded caps at the bottom, truck art above; "First Christmas for Charlotte" — green script title with the name as the last line (dedication-style "for <name>" phrasing inside the title), baby avatar with teddy; "Where's Noah?" — white bubble letters on a dark-blue circle badge over a crowd scene (near-identical device to Librio's "Where's Daisy?"); "Amelia's 1st Birthday" — pink script possessive name with balloons; "Zara Learns to Count 1-20" — blue rounded caps. — [Dinkleboo AU home](https://www.dinkleboo.com.au/); [Dinkleboo AU best-selling books](https://www.dinkleboo.com.au/collections/best-selling-books)
- Catalogue: 30 best-selling titles all "From $16.99 / $33.99" with "50% off"; filter "Avatar" (18 books); character customisation "Add name and gender", "Choose 1 parent to be in the book" — [Dinkleboo AU best-selling books](https://www.dinkleboo.com.au/collections/best-selling-books)
- Avatar policy: the company "doesn't use real photos. Instead, the 'avatar books' create an illustrated character meant to resemble the child"; dedication "printed on the first page" — [Dinkleboo.com](https://www.dinkleboo.com/)
- Customer cover/name complaints (ProductReview.com.au, 3.5/5 from 98): "The type set went to the end of the page or was squashed at times" for 6-8-letter names (Ruth, QLD); "Take care if a name is 6-8 letters you will get a squashed typeset!" (Julieanne O); "A small, low quality book" that "looks quite cheap" (Shiloh, WA); praise "Beautifully illustrated" and "large hard cover version" — [ProductReview.com.au, Dinkleboo](https://www.productreview.com.au/listings/dinkleboo)

#### Inferences
- Dinkleboo appears to use fixed-width name slots in the title lockup (hence squashing), confirming that it does not auto-reflow titles.

#### Gaps
- No announcement of closure found; the redirect is the only evidence that the storefront is gone.

## Key question 1: What are the 3-5 dominant cover templates across these competitors?

### Takeaway
Five recurring templates emerge: (1) Name-in-title over full-bleed scene (Wonderbly, I See Me, Dinkleboo, Librio Lost for Words); (2) Avatar-hero + fixed 3D title with a floating script name (Hooray Heroes); (3) Circle/porthole badge "Where's <Name>?" (Librio, Dinkleboo); (4) Small title + giant name as the hero word (My Magic Story, Librio Welcome to the World); (5) Licensed artwork with a discreet name line or "for <name>" strap (In The Book, Put Me In The Story, Hallmark).

## Key question 3: How do they handle variable-length titles/names (auto-fit, fixed layouts)?

### Takeaway
Evidence points to fixed name slots with character caps rather than true auto-fit: Librio states the name space "cannot be changed" and double names may not fit; In The Book caps names at 12 characters; I See Me! historically 15+15; Dinkleboo customers report 6-8-letter names getting "squashed"; Wonderbly is the only brand claiming "any name, however long or short" (via a partner blog), and it absorbs multiple names by extending the title line.

### Cited Findings
- "The allocated space for the name is unfortunately limited and cannot be changed." "Occasionally, double-names can be too long to fit in the space provided." — [Librio help](https://help.librio.com/article/240-why-cant-i-add-the-full-name)
- "up to 12 characters" and the name "will feature on the cover" — [What's Good To Do, In The Book Unicorn](https://whatsgoodtodo.com/my-personalised-unicorn-adventure-book-review/)
- "15 characters for first and middle names combined, and an additional 15 characters for the last name" (older I See Me! titles) — [The Old Schoolhouse via search](https://www.theoldschoolhouse.com/product-reviews/?rid=2816)
- "The type set went to the end of the page or was squashed at times" for 6-8-letter names; "Take care if a name is 6-8 letters you will get a squashed typeset!" — [ProductReview.com.au, Dinkleboo](https://www.productreview.com.au/listings/dinkleboo)
- "all of Wonderbly's story books are able to be customized for any name, however long or short" — [Sago Mini blog](https://sagomini.com/article/whats-in-a-name-5-reasons-we-love-wonderblys-customizable-storybooks/)
- Multi-name covers extend the title line: "WHERE ARE YOU ARCHIE, EMILY & FREYA?" and "CHARLIE & OLIVIA The Search for Santa" (observed) — [Wonderbly all books](https://www.wonderbly.com/uk/personalized-books); "HENRY, AMELIA & DADDY" two-kid edition vs "ENZO & DADDY" (observed) — [Librio products](https://uk.librio.com/en/products); Hooray Heroes sells separate "2 KIDS / 3 KIDS" versions of each title rather than one adaptive cover — [Hooray Heroes Lucy Rocks](https://hoorayheroes.com/products/custom-gift-for-kid)
- Wonderbly cover asset approach for a letter-driven cover: "thousands of assets," including each letter and special characters — [Creative Lives in Progress](https://www.creativelivesinprogress.com/article/adam-hancher)

### Inferences
- The industry norm is to pre-draw title words and drop the name into a bounded slot in a script/rounded face that tolerates condensation; brands avoid fully re-flowing hand-lettered titles. Brands that need multiple names ship separate SKUs (Hooray Heroes, Librio 2-kids editions) rather than auto-laying-out.

### Gaps
- No brand documents its actual fitting algorithm (scale-to-fit vs tracking vs line-break).

## Key question 4: Which brands look most premium / most generic, and why (typography, framing, colour)?
